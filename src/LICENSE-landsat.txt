landsat_farmland_poland.jpg
===========================

"Farming on the Straight, Curved, and Narrow" — the ribbon-like fields around
Sułoszowa, southern Poland, acquired by the OLI-2 on Landsat 9 on
August 13, 2024 (natural colour).
NASA Earth Observatory image by Lauren Dauphin, using Landsat data from the
U.S. Geological Survey.
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/farming-on-the-straight-curved-and-narrow-154057/

Licence: public domain. Landsat data is a work of the U.S. Government, and
NASA Earth Observatory images are free to re-publish and re-use, including
for commercial purposes, except where a copyright is indicated (none is for
this image). The only mandatory credit is NASA; the full credit line is given
here anyway so the source stays traceable.

Modified: a 1000 x 1000 px square cropped from the top of the 1152 x 1280
original (clear of a quarry at its left edge and the city at its foot) and
resized to 1024 x 1024 — a power of two, so a WebGL1 preview can tile it.

Used by the Cloud Flight generator as its default ground image
($[generator_assets]/cloud-flight/landsat_farmland_poland.jpg).
